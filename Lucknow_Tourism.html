Story Behind Bara Imambara

Bara Imambara was built in 1784 by Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula as a relief measure for famine-stricken people. It is famous for its central hall, which is one of the largest arched constructions without beams in the world. The complex also includes the Bhulbhulaiya, a labyrinth that was designed to confuse invaders. The monument is a fine example of Mughal architecture blended with local Awadhi styles.
